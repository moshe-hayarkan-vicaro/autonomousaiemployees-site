Engineering & Product AI employees

Testing, review and documentation roles that sit next to the codebase without owning it.

Engineering is the category where AI employees are most hyped and least understood. An AI employee is not a replacement engineer. It is very good at the work around the code: writing the test that nobody wrote, reviewing a diff against a checklist, keeping the docs in sync with what shipped, turning a support thread into a reproducible bug report. The roles here are all scoped that way, and all of them assume a human merges.
