Customer Support AI employees

Tier-one triage, deflection and internal IT support, where volume is high and the answer is usually already written down somewhere.

Support is the highest-volume, most measurable place to put an AI employee, and also the easiest place to do it badly. The failure mode is not wrong answers, it is confident wrong answers on the small percentage of tickets that carry real money or real anger. The roles here are scoped around triage and drafting rather than autonomous resolution, because a human approving a draft costs seconds and a wrong refund costs a customer. Teams that get this right see deflection on the boring 60 percent and better handling time on the rest.
