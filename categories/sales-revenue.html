Sales & Revenue AI employees

Research, outbound and post-sale roles where the bottleneck is time spent reading and writing rather than talking.

Sales roles split cleanly in two. The talking part, discovery and negotiation, is still human work and will be for a while. The reading and writing around it, account research, list building, follow-up drafting, renewal prep, is where the hours actually go. An AI employee is a good fit for the second half and a bad fit for the first. Every role on this page is scoped to the second half on purpose. If you deploy one of these and it starts emailing prospects unsupervised, that is a configuration mistake, not a capability.
