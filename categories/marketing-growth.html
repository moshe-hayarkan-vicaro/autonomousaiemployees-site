Marketing & Growth AI employees

Roles that produce, distribute and measure demand. The most commonly deployed AI employees, because the work is high-volume, text-heavy and already instrumented with analytics.

Marketing is where most teams start, and for a boring reason: the work is already written down. Briefs, style guides, keyword lists, campaign calendars, analytics dashboards. An AI employee can be handed a defined input and a defined output on day one, which is exactly the condition that makes delegation work. The roles below are ordered by how quickly they pay back. An SEO analyst or content writer usually shows value inside a month because the output is measurable in a tool you already own. A social media manager takes longer, because brand voice is the hard part and voice is the one thing you cannot delegate.
