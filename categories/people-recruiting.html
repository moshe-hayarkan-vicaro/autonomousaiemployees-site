People & Recruiting AI employees

Sourcing, screening logistics and onboarding coordination, where the work is scheduling and summarising rather than judging people.

Hiring is a judgement business wrapped in an administration business. The administration part, sourcing lists, scheduling, status updates, onboarding checklists, is where recruiters lose their week and where an AI employee is genuinely useful. The judgement part, who to hire, is not delegable and should not be. Every role here is deliberately scoped to the administration side. There is also a compliance dimension most teams underestimate: screening criteria and rejection reasons are regulated in many jurisdictions, so an AI employee that touches candidate decisions needs a human in the loop by design, not by policy.
