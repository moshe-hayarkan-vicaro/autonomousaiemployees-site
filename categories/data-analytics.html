Data & Analytics AI employees

Analysis, reporting and research roles where the output is a written answer to a question rather than a dashboard nobody opens.

Most analytics teams do not have a dashboard problem, they have a question-answering problem. Someone asks why a number moved, and a person spends two days pulling data to write four paragraphs. That is the gap these roles fill. The important constraint is that an AI employee should never be the system of record for a number. It reads from your warehouse, it writes prose, and every figure it states should be traceable to a query a human can re-run.
