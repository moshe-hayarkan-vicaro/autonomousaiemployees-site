Finance & Operations AI employees

Back-office roles built on documents, reconciliations and recurring reports, where correctness is checkable and the audit trail matters more than speed.

Finance work is the opposite of marketing work. The volume is lower, the tolerance for error is near zero, and every action needs to be explainable to an auditor a year later. That makes it a strange fit for an autonomous agent and an excellent fit for a supervised one. The roles below are all designed around a human sign-off step, with the AI employee doing the reading, matching and drafting that eats the week. Deploy them where a mistake is caught before it posts, never where it posts itself.
