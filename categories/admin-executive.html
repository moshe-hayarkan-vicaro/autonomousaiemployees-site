Admin & Executive AI employees

Calendar, inbox, meeting and coordination roles, the highest-frequency, lowest-glory work in any company.

Executive support is the role people most often imagine when they hear AI employee, and the one where expectations most often outrun reality. An AI employee cannot read a room. It can, however, hold a calendar, chase a room full of people for availability, write the agenda, take the notes and produce the follow-up before anyone has left the call. That is most of the job by hours. The roles here are scoped to coordination and preparation, with the relationship work left where it belongs.
